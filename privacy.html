Privacy Policy

Brand: Nickolas Pearson · Last updated October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nickolas Pearson ("I", "me", "my") collects and uses information through a private, family-only text messaging service that runs on a personal home media server (the "Service"). The Service is not a commercial or public service. It is used only by the owner and members of the owner's immediate family.

Information I collect

When someone texts the Service's phone number, I collect:

  • The mobile phone number the message was sent from.
  • The content of the text message, such as a request to add a movie.
  • The date and time the message was sent.

How I use this information

I use this information only to:

  • Recognize which family member is texting.
  • Process their request, such as adding a movie to the home media server.
  • Send a text reply confirming the result.
  • Let the owner approve or block a phone number before it can use the Service.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Messages are delivered through a messaging provider (Twilio), which transmits text messages on my behalf. The provider handles messages only to carry out delivery of the Service. No information is shared with anyone else, except where required by law.

Storage and retention

Phone numbers and message records are stored on a private server in the owner's home. I keep them only as long as needed to run the Service. I will delete a person's phone number and messages on request.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
  • To have your phone number and messages deleted, contact me using the details below.
